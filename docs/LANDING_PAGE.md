# Autonexus — Sales / Landing Page Playbook

Copy and structure for the public marketing page. Tone: **outcome-focused, plain
English, zero AI jargon**. The buyer is a dealer owner in Ireland — busy, practical,
sceptical of tech promises.

## Core message

> **Sell more cars. Waste fewer leads.**
> Autonexus is the assistant that writes your ads, answers your leads and watches your
> stock — so your team can focus on selling.

## Page structure

### 1. Hero
- H1: `Sell more cars. Waste fewer leads.`
- Sub: `The all-in-one assistant for Irish dealers — inventory, ads and leads in one place.`
- Primary CTA: `Start free trial` (14 days, no card)
- Secondary CTA: `Book a demo`
- Visual: dashboard screenshot / mockup with a DoneDeal-style ad being generated.

### 2. Pain points ("Sound familiar?")
- "A lead messaged at 9pm on Saturday. Nobody replied until Monday. They bought elsewhere."
- "You spend hours writing the same DoneDeal ad, car after car."
- "That 2018 Qashqai has been on the forecourt for 60 days and nobody noticed."
- "You're paying for ads but can't tell which ones actually sell cars."

### 3. Feature blocks (map to MVP)
1. **AI Ad Writer** — "From photos and specs to a polished DoneDeal, Facebook and
   Instagram ad in 30 seconds." Show before/after example:
   *"2019 Volkswagen Golf 1.6 TDI Highline — full service history, new NCT..."*
2. **Never miss a lead** — every enquiry lands in one pipeline; automatic first reply
   in seconds, day or night. (Stage 2 — mark as "coming soon" until shipped.)
3. **Stock that tells you what it needs** — aging alerts, price-vs-market warnings,
   status pipeline from bought to sold.
4. **Know what's working** — leads, cost per lead, conversion, profit — in one dashboard.

### 4. "Built for Ireland"
- DoneDeal & Carzone ad formats out of the box.
- NCT expiry tracking on every vehicle.
- Finance pre-qualification designed for Irish lenders (PPS-aware, GDPR-compliant).
- Data hosted in the EU.

### 5. How it works (3 steps)
1. Add your cars (2 minutes each).
2. Generate ads and share everywhere.
3. Watch leads arrive — reply, book test drives, sell.

### 6. Social proof
Placeholder for testimonials + logos. Until real ones exist, use a founder note:
*"Built with input from dealers in Dublin and Cork."* (only if true).

### 8. FAQ
- **Is my data safe?** Each dealership's data is fully isolated and hosted in the EU.
  GDPR-compliant. You own your data and can export it anytime.
- **Do I need to install anything?** No — it runs in the browser, works on your phone.
- **Does it post to DoneDeal automatically?** Ad text is generated ready to paste today;
  direct publishing integrations are on the roadmap.
- **Can I cancel?** Anytime, no contracts.

### 9. Final CTA
`Your next lead shouldn't wait until Monday.` → `Start free trial`

## SEO / channels notes
- Keywords: "dealer management software Ireland", "DoneDeal ad generator",
  "car sales CRM Ireland", "dealership CRM".
- Launch channels: Facebook groups for Irish motor trade, direct outreach to
  independent dealers, Google Ads on the keywords above.
- Landing page variants to test: lead-loss angle vs. time-saving angle.
